04 / Limits

What these counts can and cannot say

@@c5_card@@

@@disclaimer@@

  • Counts, not rates. In FDA's own words, from the openFDA documentation: MDR data alone cannot be used to establish rates of events; a causal relationship cannot be established between product and the reactions listed in a report; and every API response says to assume all results are unvalidated. A quiet code is not a safe code.
  • Summary reports. @@summary_short@@
  • The snapshot. Receipts run through @@as_of@@, as the source had loaded them on @@last_updated@@. @@chronology_snapshot@@
  • Reports without an event date. @@c5_missing@@ of the headline code's eligible reports carry none; they are counted in charts 1 to 4 and left out of chart 5 only.
  • What public data cannot supply. Exposure by lot and site, verification and validation results, the defect backlog, supplier and process measures, and complaint intake before it becomes a report.
  • What this page refuses to do. It names no manufacturer or brand, publishes no rate and no risk, and does not read the recall timeline as a validation of the rule.
